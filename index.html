01 / About

Who I am

whoami.md — read-only

I'm a student navigating the vast landscape of knowledge — from technology and science to philosophy and design. I'm not racing toward a title or a job; I'm here for the learning itself.

My approach is simple: stay curious, ask questions, and never stop exploring. Some days that means diving into a new programming concept; other days it's reading about history, debating ideas, or just thinking deeply about how things connect.

I believe the best learning happens when you follow your curiosity rather than a fixed path. This website is a small reflection of that philosophy — a space to share what I'm discovering along the way.
